## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-premium digital experience at the intersection of haute botanical apothecary and high-fashion hair couture. The aesthetic balances the intellectual restraint of modern apothecary culture with the theatrical opulence of luxury beauty ateliers.

### Aesthetic Pillars
- **Dark Decadence & Restraint:** Dominated by deep, tactile matte black surfaces that evoke obsidian glass, heavy cardstock, and darkened salon interiors.
- **Editorial Poise:** High-contrast editorial display serif typography paired with disciplined, whisper-quiet geometric sans-serif body copy.
- **Botanical Alchemy:** Accents drawn directly from rare flora—warm aged champagne foils, oxidized olive greens, and fleeting cool damascena blues.
- **Precision Craftsmanship:** Micro-hairline gold dividers, soft diffused luminescence, frosted optical glass, and generous negative space reminiscent of oversized luxury monographs.

### Emotional Experience
The user interface should feel calm, sensorial, and deliberately unhurried. Interactions must evoke weight, permanence, and bespoke luxury rather than fast-paced digital commerce.

## Colors

The palette operates in a default dark mode, constructing an immersive, dimly lit atelier atmosphere where product formulations and metallic accents gleam under focused light.

### Color Tokens & Usage
- **Primary (`#C9A24B` - Champagne Gold):** Reserved for prestige focal points, brand marks, key CTA borders, active selection states, and delicate hairline accents. A softer tint (`#DFC07A`) serves as hover illumination and subtle glowing edges.
- **Secondary (`#4A5D23` - Olive Noir):** Evokes raw botanical essences and pressed oils. Used for organic certification badges, botanical ingredient callouts, botanical ingredient overlays, and subtle ambient gradients.
- **Tertiary (`#5B7DB1` - Damascena Blue):** A serene, crystalline accent representing hydration, delicate Nigella damascena extracts, and ritual step markers. Used sparingly for interactive micro-moments and specialized scientific disclosures.
- **Neutral Primary (`#0B0B0B` - Obsidian Base):** The primary canvas background, creating deep non-reflective contrast.
- **Neutral Surface (`#121212` - Basalt Tint):** Surface tier for cards, elevated drawers, and navigation panels.
- **Typography Base (`#F5F1E8` - Linen White):** A warm, unbleached off-white providing effortless readability without the harsh contrast of pure digital white.
- **Muted Typography (`#9E9A90` - Alabaster Mist):** Secondary information, metadata, batch numbers, and input placeholders.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy is defined by dramatic contrast: the romantic, high-contrast serifs of **Playfair Display** anchor headlines, narrative storytelling, and product names; while the rational, structural sans-serif **Manrope** provides immaculate legibility for e-commerce utility, ingredients, and clinical efficacy data.

### Typographic Rules
- **Headline Rhythm:** Editorial serifs should never be rendered in heavy weights; rely on `400` and `500` to maintain delicate hairline stems.
- **Uppercase Tracking:** All metadata tags, button labels, price indications, and section eyebrows use `label-caps` with wide letter-spacing (`0.18em`) and strict uppercase transformation.
- **Editorial Emphasis:** Italic variations in Playfair Display are reserved for botanical binomial nomenclature (e.g., *Nigella sativa*, *Camellia oleifera*) and key sensory descriptors.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors high-end editorial book layout, prioritizing generous breathing room over dense packing.

### Grid & Structure
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Fixed max-width container at `1440px` centered with fluid margins, standard column gutter of `1.5rem` (`24px`), and outer canvas margins of `4rem` (`64px`).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` margins.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Fluid edge-to-edge layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins.

### Spatial Rhythm
Sections transition with substantial vertical intervals (`space-xl` to double `space-xl`), creating a curated pacing where each product formulation or botanical essay is consumed as a discrete sensory gallery.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on harsh drop shadows; instead, it uses dark translucent glass surfaces, subtle backdrops, and metallic light dispersion.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Base Floor (`#0B0B0B`):** Canvas background with zero elevation.
2. **Low Tier (Glass Paneling):** `rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.65)` with `16px` backdrop-blur and a `1px` border of `rgba(201, 162, 75, 0.15)`. Used for content cards, routine carousels, and ingredient drawers.
3. **Mid Tier (Hover & Floating Elements):** `rgba(22, 22, 22, 0.85)` with `24px` backdrop-blur, accompanied by a subtle ambient radial glow: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`, bordered by `rgba(201, 162, 75, 0.3)`.
4. **High Tier (Overlays & Modals):** `rgba(11, 11, 11, 0.94)` with `32px` backdrop-blur, a full hairline border of `rgba(201, 162, 75, 0.25)`, and a deep peripheral vignette.

### Metallic Edge Accents
Elevated containers can feature a hairline top edge with a linear gradient moving from `transparent` to `#DFC07A` (at 40% opacity) and back to `transparent`, simulating light grazing against gold foil bevels.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, architectural shape language (`roundedness: 1`). Corners are subtly softened rather than aggressively rounded or entirely sharp, preserving the physical impression of heavy luxury packaging, perfume flacons, and cut-crystal apothecary jars.

### Corner Rules
- **Base Components (Cards, Inputs, Buttons):** `0.25rem` (`4px`) corner radius.
- **Containers & Flyouts (Drawers, Modals):** `0.5rem` (`8px`) corner radius (`rounded-lg`).
- **Pill Exceptions:** Form selector chips and sensory tags may strictly use pill shapes (`9999px`) only when indicating tactile, pickable ingredients or filter states.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Matte Obsidian background (`#121212`) framed in a `1px` solid champagne gold border (`#C9A24B`), text set in `label-caps` in `#F5F1E8`. On hover, the border illuminates to `#DFC07A` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(201, 162, 75, 0.25)`) and slight background lightening.
- **Secondary CTA:** Completely transparent background, `1px` border of `rgba(245, 241, 232, 0.2)`, text in `#F5F1E8`. On hover, border transitions to `rgba(201, 162, 75, 0.6)`.
- **Text / Editorial Link:** Warm off-white with an underlined hairline offset by `4px`. On hover, underline transforms into champagne gold and glides smoothly.

### Cards
- **Product & Story Cards:** Constructed on the Low Tier glass substrate (`rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.65)`). Borders are `1px` stroke of `rgba(201, 162, 75, 0.12)`. Padding follows `space-md` on mobile and `space-lg` on desktop. Images are displayed with muted contrast, warming on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Botanical Badges:** Olive noir tinted pill (`rgba(74, 93, 35, 0.2)`) with a delicate hairline border (`#4A5D23`) and typography in `#F5F1E8`.
- **Hydration / Alchemy Badges:** Subtle Damascena blue tint (`rgba(91, 125, 177, 0.15)`) with an accent border of `#5B7DB1`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Input Fields:** Flush, low-profile elements with `rgba(18, 18, 18, 0.8)` background and bottom-accented or minimal hairline border in `rgba(245, 241, 232, 0.2)`. On focus, the border shifts to `#C9A24B` with an ethereal gold glow. Typography uses `body-md` in `#F5F1E8`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Selectors:** Custom-crafted minimal geometric controls (`16px`). Inactive state is a sheer box with an off-white border. Active state features a solid gold fill (`#C9A24B`) with a deep matte obsidian mark (`#0B0B0B`).

### Bespoke Domain Components
- **Olfactory Pyramid Display:** A tiered visual component displaying Top, Heart, and Base notes using hairline horizontal dividing rules and Playfair Display typography.
- **Formula Concentric Dial:** A sensory dosage indicator with gold botanical measurements guiding application volume (pipette drops / pumps).
